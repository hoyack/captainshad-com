About the manual

Practical judgment, written down

Why Captain Shad's Field Manual publishes practical leadership frameworks without costume or invented exploits.

Why this exists

Leadership without costume.

Captain Shad's Field Manual is an editorial project about practical judgment: how people make decisions, brief teams, revise plans, and learn after action.

The “captain” is a point of view, not a fabricated résumé. We publish original frameworks and clearly label examples, sponsors, affiliate relationships, and future commercial offers. We do not invent expeditions, client outcomes, or authority we cannot substantiate.

Editorial standard

Every dispatch should leave the reader with a usable question, template, or practice. Corrections are logged openly. Advice is general educational material, not legal, medical, financial, or safety certification.

Publishing compass

Useful over impressive

A page should help someone make or communicate a decision.

Specific over cinematic

No stock heroics, fake exploits, or empty “captain” theater.

Revisable over absolute

Frameworks name assumptions and invite correction.
