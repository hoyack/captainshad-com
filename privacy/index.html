Privacy

A short, readable privacy policy

How Captain Shad handles subscriber and inquiry data.

Effective: July 12, 2026

What we collect

When you subscribe or send an inquiry, we collect the information you submit, such as name, email, role, organization, and message. Basic hosting logs may include device, browser, referring page, and IP address.

Why we use it

We use data to deliver requested field notes, respond to inquiries, protect forms from abuse, measure site performance, and improve the publication. We do not sell personal data.

Processors and retention

Hosting and forms may be processed by Netlify. Email delivery and privacy-respecting analytics providers will be named here before activation. We retain subscriber data until unsubscribe or deletion request, and inquiry records only as long as operationally necessary.

Your choices

Unsubscribe through any email. To request access, correction, or deletion, email privacy@captainshad.com.

Changes

Material changes will update the effective date and, where appropriate, be announced to subscribers.
