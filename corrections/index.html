Policy

Corrections should leave a trail

How to report an error and how material changes to operational guidance are documented.

Effective July 19, 2026Output: Correction recordPrint-friendly

Report a problem

Email hello@hoyack.com with the page URL, the disputed sentence or checklist item, why it may be wrong, and any supporting source or reproduction detail.

Review process

Hoyack reviews factual, safety, security, policy, accessibility, and link issues. Small copy edits may be corrected directly. A material change that alters operational advice will be noted below with the date and scope.

Correction log

July 19, 2026: Small Crew Runbooks launched as a complete replacement for the prior site concept. No prior editorial claims or personas were carried into this resource.

Limits

A disagreement in preference is not necessarily an error. Corrections do not reveal confidential reports, personal data, security-sensitive details, or privileged communications.
