How to report and review factual errors in Captain Shad field notes.

Accuracy matters more than preserving a clean-looking record. Send a correction with the page URL, disputed passage, and supporting source to editor@captainshad.com.

Review process

  1. We acknowledge actionable reports.
  2. We check the original source and relevant evidence.
  3. Material corrections are changed in place and logged below with date and explanation.
  4. Disputed judgments may receive an editor's note rather than silent removal.
