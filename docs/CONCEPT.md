# One-page concept brief

## Audience and problem

Captain Shad serves team leads, founders, operators, and advisers who make consequential calls with incomplete information. They do not need more leadership slogans; they need compact methods they can use in the next brief, decision, or debrief.

## Positioning

**Field notes for people who make the call.** A leadership/adventure publication grounded in practical judgment rather than “captain” cosplay. Every piece must supply a question, template, or operating practice.

## Information architecture

1. Homepage: promise, three starting frameworks, latest dispatch, conversion paths.
2. Manual: reusable decision, briefing, risk, debrief, and memory frameworks.
3. Dispatches: original editorial field notes.
4. Subscribe: email capture for new notes and future guides.
5. Speaking: qualified consulting/workshop inquiry.
6. About and policy pages: authority boundary, disclosures, correction path.

## Visual direction

A working field manual crossed with a coastal chart: deep navigation navy, signal orange, paper stock, coordinate grid, brass compass details, editorial serif type, and visible reference numbers. No fake expedition photography or generic SaaS gradients. All graphics are original.

## Content pillars

- Decision quality under uncertainty
- Briefing and delegated intent
- Debriefing and organizational learning
- Operating cadence and communication
- Tools and reading, only with explicit commercial disclosure

## Calls to action

Primary: **Get the field notes.** Secondary: **Explore speaking & consulting.** Conversion events are a confirmed subscription and a qualified inquiry.

## Revenue hypothesis

Consulting/workshops first; premium printable guides second; selective sponsors and clearly labeled affiliate gear later. Revenue must follow editorial trust, not interrupt it.
