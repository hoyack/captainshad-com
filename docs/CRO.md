# CRO record

## ICP

Primary: founders, managers, operators, and client advisers with decision rights and teams of roughly 3–100. Their problem is slow escalation, vague briefs, repeated mistakes, or postmortems that create blame instead of learning.

## Offer and conversion events

- Free offer: original field notes and print-ready decision templates.
- Primary conversion: successful `field-notes` Netlify form submission.
- Commercial conversion: successful `speaking-inquiry` submission containing a real decision/session need.
- Future paid offers: premium guide bundle, facilitated workshop, advisory engagement.

## Revenue mechanism

1. Consulting, facilitated workshops, and speaking.
2. Premium guides and team licenses after subscriber demand is demonstrated.
3. Select sponsors and affiliate gear with disclosure adjacent to every commercial placement.

No active affiliate or sponsor relationship is implied at launch.

## Funnel

Search/referral → framework or dispatch → subscribe → useful recurring notes → guide/workshop offer. High-intent visitors may move directly from the homepage to speaking/consulting.

## 30/60/90 distribution plan

- Days 0–30: publish one dispatch weekly; excerpt each into LinkedIn/X posts; invite an existing operator network; baseline route-to-form and completion rates.
- Days 31–60: ship a downloadable one-page decision brief; create two partner newsletter swaps; test framework-led vs dispatch-led subscription copy.
- Days 61–90: host one live decision clinic; offer the first paid team workshop; interview five subscribers; decide whether a premium guide has sufficient demand.

## Guardrails

No fabricated biography, client outcome, testimonial, expedition, or performance claim. No monetization spam. CMO approves creative/editorial changes; CRO approves offer and conversion changes; CTO approves tracking, processors, and production form routing.
