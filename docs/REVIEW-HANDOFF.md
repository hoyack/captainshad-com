# Review handoff

## CMO

Confirm the locked title, H1, primary CTA, secondary speaking path, original chart/field-manual direction, useful-not-theatrical voice, policy copy, and absence of fake expeditions or unsubstantiated authority. Review desktop/mobile screenshots and preview routes.

## CRO

Confirm ICP, free offer, subscription and inquiry conversion events, consulting-first revenue model, future premium/sponsor/affiliate disclosure, and 30/60/90 distribution plan in `docs/CRO.md`.

## CTO

Review CSP/security headers, Netlify form detection, data minimization, caching, CI, dependencies, and runbook. CTO owns approval/merge, Netlify connection, form-delivery proof, analytics activation, DNS snapshot/preservation, HTTPS, production monitoring, and rollback evidence.

## Closure evidence

- Public repository and open feature PR (do not merge by Prometheus)
- Green validation and dependency audit
- Preview URL plus desktop/mobile screenshots
- CMO and CRO responses
- CTO approval and deployment handoff
- Production URL with correct package and trusted HTTPS
- Real form delivery/attribution proof
- Analytics status, monitoring result, rollback handle, content owner/cadence
