# Continuous content loop

## Owner and cadence

- Owner: Captain Shad editor, accountable to Hoyack CMO.
- Weekly: one original dispatch or substantial manual revision.
- Monthly: one reusable template/checklist and one corrections/freshness review.
- Quarterly: pillar, funnel, disclosure, source, and stale-page audit.

## Source lanes

- Primary: documented operating observations, interviews with permission, original workshop notes, and public first-party reports.
- Reference: books, standards, peer-reviewed work, and reputable institutional research.
- Product/gear: manufacturer specifications plus independent testing where available; commercial relationship never counts as evidence.

Sources must be logged in the draft with access date. Personal anecdotes must be permissioned or anonymized. AI may assist structure and editing but may not invent facts, quotes, experience, or citations.

## Dispatch template

1. Decision or operating tension
2. Observable facts and assumptions
3. Framework or practice
4. Small worked example, clearly labeled
5. Limits and failure conditions
6. One field question or template
7. Sources and disclosure

## Approval points

- Editor: evidence, usefulness, links, accessibility, and correction readiness.
- CMO: voice, visual system, claims, headline, and publication approval.
- CRO: CTA, offer, sponsor, affiliate, or monetization changes.
- CTO: new scripts, analytics, processors, forms, redirects, or deployment changes.

## Freshness metrics

Track: days since substantive update, broken-link count, pages reviewed in the last 90 days, corrections open/closed, source age, organic entries, dispatch-to-subscribe rate, unsubscribe rate, and qualified inquiry rate. Flag practical guidance after 180 days and commercial/tool claims after 90 days.
